AC AND DC RELATIONSHIPS
Vrms = Vpeak / √2
I = V / R   ·   Pavg = Vrms² / R

For a sinusoidal AC supply, RMS voltage is its equivalent heating-effect voltage. For an ideal DC supply, its constant voltage equals its RMS value.
