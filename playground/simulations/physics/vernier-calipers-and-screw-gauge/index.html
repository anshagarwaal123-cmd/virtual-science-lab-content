Precision and error concepts

Quick reference
LEAST COUNT
The smallest measurement an instrument can resolve. A smaller least count means finer scale resolution, but does not by itself guarantee accuracy.
ZERO ERROR
A non-zero reading when the instrument should read zero with its jaws or spindle closed. Positive error is subtracted; negative error is added.
ZERO CORRECTION
Zero correction = − zero error. Corrected reading = observed reading + zero correction.

Scientific model note: This educational lab uses a 10-division Vernier scale with 1 mm main-scale divisions and a 0.1 mm least count, and a micrometer with a 0.5 mm pitch and 50 circular divisions, giving a 0.01 mm least count. The reference objects have fixed ideal dimensions. Readings are rounded to the instrument's least count. Real instruments may have different scale layouts, calibration values, backlash, contact force and measurement uncertainty. Use a gentle ratchet force on a real micrometer; do not overtighten delicate objects.
