FARADAY'S LAW OF ELECTROMAGNETIC INDUCTIONAs the coil rotates, magnetic flux linked with it changes. This induces an EMF across the coil.
FLUX AND INDUCED EMF
Φ = B A cos θ   |   ε = −N dΦ/dt

For uniform B and constant angular speed: ε = N B A ω sin θ. θ is the angle between the coil's normal and the magnetic field. The sign indicates polarity; the DC model rectifies the output using a split-ring commutator.

Scientific model note: This ideal generator uses Faraday's law for a single rotating coil in a uniform magnetic field, with constant angular speed. The AC mode represents slip-ring connections. The DC mode is an ideal full-polarity reversal by a split-ring commutator, giving a pulsating unidirectional output; it is not a smoothed DC supply. Coil resistance, brush losses, magnetic non-uniformity, inductance and mechanical drag are not included. The load current is calculated using an ideal resistive load and is zero when the external circuit is open.
