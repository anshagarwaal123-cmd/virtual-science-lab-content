🔍 What should you discover?

Keep resistance fixed and increase voltage. Current increases in the same proportion. Now keep voltage fixed and increase resistance. Current decreases.

Try doubling the voltage while keeping resistance unchanged. What happens to the current?

🎯 Quick challenge
