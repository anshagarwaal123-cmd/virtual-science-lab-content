Right-hand thumb rule

Hold the straight current-carrying conductor in your right hand. Point your thumb in the direction of conventional current. Your curled fingers show the direction of the circular magnetic field lines. In this top view, current coming out of the page (•) gives anticlockwise field lines, while current going into the page (×) gives clockwise field lines.

B = μ₀ I / (2πr)

For a long straight wire in air, μ₀ ≈ 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A. The model uses the equivalent convenient form B(µT) = 20 × I(A) / r(cm).
