What should you discover?

The eye lens changes its focal length to focus light from objects at different distances on the retina. In myopia, distant objects may focus in front of the retina; a concave (diverging) lens can correct this. In hypermetropia, near objects may focus behind the retina; a convex (converging) lens can help correct it. This activity uses a simplified equivalent thin-lens model.

Lens power (D) = 1 / focal length (m)
