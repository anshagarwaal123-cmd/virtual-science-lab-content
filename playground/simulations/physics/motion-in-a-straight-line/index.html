INSTANTANEOUS MOTIONThe object moves in the positive direction at constant velocity. Acceleration is zero.
EQUATIONS OF MOTION · CONSTANT ACCELERATION
v = u + at   ·   x = x₀ + ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2a(x − x₀)

These equations apply when acceleration is constant. Displacement is x − x₀; distance travelled is the total path length and is never negative.

Free exploration

Scientific model note: The simulation uses one-dimensional motion with constant acceleration and the standard kinematic equations. Distance travelled is calculated by accounting for a possible reversal at the instant velocity becomes zero. The track is a visual representation; the numerical values and graphs are calculated in SI units. The model does not include friction or air resistance unless represented by a chosen constant acceleration.
