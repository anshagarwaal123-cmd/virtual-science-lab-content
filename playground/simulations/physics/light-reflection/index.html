What are you observing?

When a light ray strikes a plane mirror, it reflects from the surface. The angle of incidence and angle of reflection are measured from the normal, which is perpendicular to the mirror at the point where the light ray strikes it. In this ideal plane-mirror model, these two angles are always equal.

Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection
