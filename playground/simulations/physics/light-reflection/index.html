What should you discover?

The angle of incidence is the angle between the incoming ray and the normal. The angle of reflection is the angle between the reflected ray and the normal. In an ideal plane-mirror model, the reflected ray is symmetric to the incident ray about the normal. Do not measure either angle from the mirror surface itself.

Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection
