HOW THE MOTOR ROTATESCurrent in the coil interacts with the magnetic field. The split-ring commutator reverses coil current every half-turn so the torque keeps the rotor turning in the same mechanical direction.
MAGNETIC TORQUE ON A COIL

θ is the angle between the coil's normal and magnetic field. The model uses the average value of |sin θ| (2/π) for its simplified rotating-motor dynamics.

One electrical cycle

Model note: This is an educational idealised DC motor. The displayed rotation and speed are produced by a simplified torque, back-EMF and load model. Real motors also have winding inductance, brush voltage drop, friction, iron losses, heating, magnetic saturation and detailed electromagnetic transients, which are not fully modelled here. The split-ring commutator reverses the current in the rotating coil every half-turn.
