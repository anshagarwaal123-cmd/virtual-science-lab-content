What should you discover?

When light crosses a boundary between transparent media, its direction changes because its speed changes. The angle of incidence and angle of refraction are measured from the normal. For a given pair of media, the relationship is described by Snell’s law. At a higher-to-lower refractive-index boundary, sufficiently large incidence angles produce total internal reflection.

n₁ sin(i) = n₂ sin(r)
