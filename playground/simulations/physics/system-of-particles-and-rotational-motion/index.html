Centre of Mass of Two Particles

Change masses and positions to find the balance point of a two-particle system.

LIVE MODEL
Idealised one-dimensional system · SI units
5.0 kgm₁ + m₂
4.0 mxCM
2.0 kgm₁
3.0 kgm₂

LIVE EQUATION

xCM = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) / (m₁ + m₂)
xCM = 4.0 m

WHAT TO NOTICE

The centre of mass lies closer to the heavier particle. It is the mass-weighted average position of the system.
