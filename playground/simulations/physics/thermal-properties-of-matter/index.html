Principle of calorimetry
Heat lost = Heat gained

Σ(mcT) / Σ(mc) gives the equilibrium temperature for an isolated system. The calorimeter heat capacity is included.

Guided mission

Find the balance point

Mix 0.20 kg of hot water at 90°C with 0.40 kg of cold water at 20°C. Predict the final temperature, then run the experiment.

Heat balance · ideal isolated system

Understanding the result

In an ideal insulated calorimeter, the energy lost by the hotter body equals the energy gained by the colder body and calorimeter. The final temperature lies between the initial temperatures.
