FLEMING'S LEFT HAND RULE Thumb = Force · First finger = Field · Middle finger = Current
FORCE ON A CURRENT-CARRYING CONDUCTOR
F = B I L sin θ

B = magnetic flux density (T), I = current (A), L = length in field (m), θ = angle between current and magnetic field.

Scientific model note: This experiment calculates the magnetic force on a straight conductor in a uniform magnetic field using F = BIL sin θ. The display uses a simplified 2D projection: the conductor and magnetic field lie in the screen plane, while the force is represented into or out of the screen. Real laboratory setups may also involve contact forces, gravity and circuit effects, which are not included here.
